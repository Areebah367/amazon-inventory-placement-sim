Where the sites serve

Dots are delivery areas, coloured by the Forward Site profile they are grouped into. Area groupings are illustrative, not verified Amazon facilities or service boundaries. Coverage is calculated per Forward Site, so areas on the same site share its figure.

Use the same decision rule in the next city

  1. Collect daily category demand, packaged item sizes, usable Forward Site space and the replenishment allowance.
  2. Apply the same category protection and allocation rule.
  3. Review coverage, cost and stockouts; update inputs as local order data becomes available.

Every Forward Site uses the same data structure (space, replenishment cycle, inbound allowance and daily demand by category), so a new site needs data, not a new algorithm. You can try this with Add Forward Site in the scenario editor.

  • The demo uses seven hypothetical site profiles and eight product categories. They are not a list of Amazon facilities and do not represent one site per emirate.
  • Local coverage is units stocked at Forward Sites divided by expected demand over each site's replenishment cycle. It is not an on-time delivery rate or order-level availability.
  • Network terms: Central Fulfilment Centres hold the full range, Forward Sites hold the recommended stock, and Delivery Stations hold no inventory in this model.
  • Monthly holding cost is a proxy: stocked volume × 0.5 (average stock) × 35.31 ft³/m³ × the scenario's $/ft³/month rate.
  • Not connected to any Amazon or AWS system.
